# Moha Motors — Quick Guide

A short manual for visitors and admins.

---

## For Visitors

### Finding a vehicle
1. Click **Inventory** (or **Browse Cars**) in the top menu.
2. Use the **sidebar filters** to narrow results:
   - **Search by name or brand** — e.g. `Toyota`, `Prado`, `Vitz`
   - **Budget range**, **Brand**, **Category**, **Condition**, **Transmission**, **Year**
3. Click any car to open its **full details** (photos, specs, price).

### Saving vehicles (♥)
- Tap the **heart** on any car (card or detail page) to save it.
- View saved cars via the **heart icon** in the top menu → **Saved Vehicles**.
- Saved cars are kept on your device for **24 hours**, then clear automatically.

### Making an enquiry
- On a car's page, click **Chat on WhatsApp** or **Send Inquiry** to fill the contact form.
- You can also use the **Contact** page for general questions.

---

## For Admins

### Signing in
1. Go to **`/admin/login`**.
2. Enter your email and password (use the 👁 icon to reveal what you typed).
3. You land on the **Dashboard** (overview of inventory & inquiries).

### Navigating
The left sidebar has: **Dashboard · Inventory · Brands · Categories · Inquiries · Admins · Profile**.

### Adding a new vehicle
1. Go to **Inventory** → click **Add Vehicle** (`/admin/vehicles/new`).
2. Fill in the fields. **Required:** Title, Brand, Category, Year, Price, Color, Fuel Type, Engine Size, Seats, Transmission.
   - **Fuel Type** and **Transmission** start blank — you must choose them; there is no default.
   - Brand or Category missing? Add it first under **Brands** / **Categories**.
3. **Add photos** — select one or more images (the first becomes the main photo).
4. Click **Save**. You'll be returned to the inventory list.

> Editing a car: open **Inventory** → click a vehicle → change fields or images → **Save**.
> Mark a car sold by turning off **Available**.

### Adding a brand or category
1. Open **Brands** (or **Categories**).
2. Type the name in the box at the top and click **Add**.
3. Edit or delete existing entries from the list below.

### Managing inquiries
- Open **Inquiries** to read customer messages, mark them read/resolved, or delete them.

### Adding another admin
1. Open **Admins**.
2. Enter the new admin's name, email, and password → **Create Admin**.

### Your account
- Open **Profile** to update your name/email or **change your password**.
- Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar when finished.

---

*Tip: every save shows a confirmation message. If something fails, an error message explains what to fix.*
